## Departure authority and retained ownership

Movement settlement now records every moving member as outside before choosing
any successor. It then ends employment and personal civic bindings, refreshes
business/project/estate authority, and reconciles legal representation inside
the same outer savepoint. A closure or succession failure rolls back residence,
membership, roles, assignments, events and financial state together.

- `CivicAuthority.release_for_departure` records the original personal role,
  legislative office, agency leadership, staff/task assignment and personal
  permit/counsel bindings in movement-linked events. It releases local office,
  abandons pending personal applications and revokes unused personal permits.
  Paid fees and approved institutional decisions retain their history. It does
  not invoke death settlement. Return does not automatically restore an office.
- City staff selection excludes outside candidates and preserves a vacancy
  until an existing eligible resident can fill it. The tests exercise a clerk's
  actual pending task, an outside candidate and that same candidate's return;
  this produces neither a new person nor a new monetary grant.
- Business successors must be available residents. Shares and founder identity
  remain unchanged; the next qualified shareholder, estate representative,
  guardian or employee operates the firm, otherwise control is vacant. A sole
  owner's return can resolve that vacancy. Property refresh also seeds control
  history for projects that have never been inherited, so their original
  owner's departure cannot leave a misleading current-owner fallback.
- Estate beneficiary fractions and assets remain owned by the recorded people.
  A living outside beneficiary cannot exercise local administration. Existing
  eligible public trustees can serve; otherwise the estate records a vacancy.
  This eligibility filter does not remove the beneficiary's financial interest.
- An outside person cannot take a new local legal action. A previously accepted
  resident counsel mandate may continue for that client's own personal rights.
  An unaccepted request or an organizational mandate whose requester loses
  authority ends. Departing counsel loses current mandates. Independent legal
  source checks use residence at the action/consent event frontier, preserving
  valid earlier filings and rejecting an outside filing even when a test fault
  disables action admission.

## Local commitments on departure

`engine/population_commitments.py` coordinates owning-service cancellation in
the movement's existing savepoint, after residence is recorded and before local
authority is released. The chosen draft policy is
`end_local_commitments_retain_assets_v1`. It ends personal local access and
unfilled requests without issuing refunds, distributing assets or changing
the parties to an existing debt. This is a declared model policy.

| Commitment | Recorded departure effect | Financial/history boundary |
|---|---|---|
| City occupancy | Cancel active occupancy leases and record `ended_tick` through `local_occupancy` endings | Property ownership remains; returned residents need new occupancy intervals |
| Insurance | Active policy becomes cancelled with an end day | Paid premiums and claims remain; later premiums stop; return requires new cover |
| Compute access | Pending/active personal, employer-paid or publicly paid access is cancelled; tier becomes local | Paid charges remain; no replacement subscription or automatic restoration on return |
| Personal loan application | Pending request expires with its decision day | Disbursed loans keep their repayment schedule; company applications remain with the company |
| Internal migration | Pending local relocation is cancelled with its completion day | The person cannot be moved internally after leaving; existing wallets and balances remain |
| Equity, FX and IPO bids | Open orders, including a partial equity order's remainder, are cancelled | Executed fills and allocated shares remain; cancellation creates no cash refund |
| Estate property/private-share bids | The owning market appends a buyer-unavailable ending | Title, retained estate assets and creditor rights remain; the old bid stays ended after return |

The new draft `population_commitment_endings` table binds every listed service
or order ending to its pending group, person, due day, prior residence event and
exact event payload. Update/delete guards preserve the original source snapshot
and permitted terminal changes. The independent reader rejects changed holders,
payment terms, reactivation, missing event records and outside people with open
local commitments. Settlement and terminal retries run that reader. A later
failure rolls back all group effects; Store-close/reopen preserves the endings
and retry does not repeat them.

Estate markets retain their existing immutable ending tables. Their new
historical eligibility reader uses residence at the ending's event frontier,
so a later return cannot invalidate an earlier departure. Death settlement
opens an estate before it closes bids and appends its final death event. The
reader accepts that preceding, source-bound estate opening while rejecting an
opening that was not yet present at the selected boundary. Legacy semantics
keep their original death-based eligibility rule.

## Declared population schedules

`engine/population_scenario.py` connects prospective experiment inputs to the
same movement service used by the domain tests. It is enabled only by the

The complete schedule is parsed before World construction effects. It is
declared at genesis in `population_scenario_manifest`, bound to a canonical
event, and checked against both the runtime configuration and saved run
configuration. Restart rejects edited schedules or missing declarations. Each
accepted or rejected input has an immutable `population_scenario_receipts` row
and matching event. A committed World phase cannot omit its scheduled input
and then silently execute it late.

This example describes the draft input format; ordinary runs still reject
Semantics 21 until the remaining integration is complete. The `agent:23` key

```yaml
  population_mode: drift
population:

    - key: out
      tick: 0
      action: propose
      actor: agent:23

      tick: 3
      action: propose
      actor: agent:23
      cause: return
      members: [agent:23]
      destination_region_id: 1
      due_tick: 5
```

Inputs use unique keys and recorded person-origin strings. Birth and arrival
keys resolve only after their registration event; names and changing list
positions are never selectors. The schedule supports at most 1,024 inputs,
64-character input keys, 256-character person keys and 128 members/care
dispositions per proposal. Unknown fields, ambiguous structure, duplicate
keys/members, invalid days and unsupported causes are rejected before any
World effects. An expression index over registration/birth events supports
origin lookup without scanning unrelated event history.

Every proposal specifies `key`, `tick`, `action: propose`, `actor`, `cause`,
`members` and a later `due_tick`. Returns also specify
`destination_region_id`. For every affected minor, `care_plan` lists an
explicit `child` origin and `guardian` origin; moving members and remaining
members must satisfy the domain service's household/custody rules.

Each other affected adult must have a separate response input with `key`,
`tick`, `action: respond`, `actor`, `proposal` (the proposal input's key) and
`decision: accept`, `decline` or `withdraw`. Responses must fall between the
proposal's submission day and due day. A proposal records only its own actor's
assent. Decline or withdrawal cancels the agreement through the existing
service; absence of another adult's assent prevents settlement.

Day-zero submissions occur after genesis household/person creation. On later
days, ordinary life events and scheduled births run first, then scenario
inputs, then due movement settlement, before local benefits and activity.
For each day, proposals precede responses, with stable input-key order within
each group; rearranging YAML entries does not change that order. The existing
first-arrival service runs later in the nightly phase, so an arrival can first
be selected by this schedule on a subsequent input day. A return changes the
same person's residence; it does not create another arrival or endowment.

Invalid requests are recorded as rejections. An origin that does not yet exist
is rejected on that input day and remains rejected if the person arrives
later. Corrupt/missing residence evidence or an origin identifying multiple
people raises a source error. Such an error or a failed receipt rolls back the
whole input batch; the World savepoint also rolls back the nightly phase.
Exact retry does not duplicate proposals, assents or receipts. The audit binds
accepted inputs to their movement terms and to the result at the receipt's
event frontier, so later settlement cannot rewrite an earlier pending result.

## Economic contract

- Life status and modeled residence are independent. A departure preserves the
  same agent/person ID, immutable origin and birth date. It does not invoke an
  estate, create an arrival persona or issue another endowment on return.
- Every residence change has immutable event provenance, a prior-state identity
  and an idempotency key. Historical reads use the selected day and, when
  necessary, the event frontier within that day. A later return cannot change an
  earlier census or inspection.
- Local work, consumption, care delivery, appointments, new applications,
  governance, model scheduling and map presence exclude outside residents.
  Financial ownership queries continue to include them. Replacing every
  `alive=1` predicate would confuse these two uses and is not an implementation.
- Existing deposits, loans, beneficial shares, property interests and creditor
  rights retain their owners. Departure alone transfers no money or assets.
  Scheduled interest, contractual dues and later receipts continue under their
  existing contracts. The first population protocol explicitly retains assets
  in the modeled financial system; it reports zero cross-boundary cash transfer
  rather than pretending those assets were liquidated or exported. A future
  capital-transfer instrument requires its own currency-specific ledger legs,
  bank treatment and return/estate rules; it cannot be inferred from migration.
- Personal work and civic authority cannot remain occupied by an unavailable
  actor. End affected assignments with recorded reasons, retain earned claims,
  cancel stale offers/consents, and use the existing qualified succession and
  vacancy rules. Ownership does not imply authority to operate a business.
- A departing household needs a recorded care/residence disposition for every
  affected minor. Adult assent and current membership/custody snapshots govern
  the group. No adult gains custody or obligor status merely by remaining nearby.
  Outside household relationships may persist, but they deliver no local care.
- The outside-life policy retains the declared age, illness/recovery and
  baseline/illness mortality mechanisms. Outside onset uses the baseline
  hazard without local epidemic shocks. Local medical consumption and births
  are excluded; no foreign medical economy is inferred. Same-day death and
  any invalidated care/assent snapshot cancel a pending movement.

## Scheduling and ordering

Open migration is a prospective scenario/decision regime, distinct from stable
adult replacement and closed endogenous drift. Keep the older modes unchanged.
Scenario schedules and accepted household requests identify people by recorded
origin or existing ID, never by a mutable list position. A return refers to the
existing person and requires an outside state; ordinary first arrivals keep
their separately recorded population funding.

For the new regime, resolve ordinary aging/deaths and births first, then due
external movements before local benefits, family matching, civic presence and
time/production allocation. Revalidate current household membership, adulthood,
guardianship and each required assent immediately before atomic settlement.
Record cancellations when those terms changed. Later same-day deaths are
classified using residence immediately before the death event, not the final
state of a different day.

Cancel local pending actions and release encumbrances through their owning
services, within the same savepoint as the group's residence/history changes.
Do not reuse death settlement: a surviving outside owner still owns their
claims. Re-entry establishes an authorized local household/region without
recreating accounts, social identity, birth records or opening funds.

## History and census

`person_residence_events` records one append-only state chain per person.
Its event link, predecessor, cause, selected tick, request identity and ledger
frontier are checked independently. Origins are tied to `person_lifecycle`.
The schema prevents divergent chains and mutation/deletion of evidence.

Keep the existing all-living-person census contract intact. Add a separately
named resident census with this identity:

```text
closing residents = opening residents + resident births + first arrivals
                  + returns + other resident entries - departures
                  - deaths while resident
known living people = closing residents + known living outside
```

Returning people are not first arrivals. Outside deaths reduce known living
people but not resident deaths. An outside person is not absent from the
financial owner inventory. Source corruption or missing supported history must
be unavailable/error evidence, not a guessed resident status. Genesis has a
declared opening stock rather than being counted as migration.
