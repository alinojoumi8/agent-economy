## Implemented behavior

- Direct federal elections now roll back seat or executive-metric changes,
  election rows and receipts together after a late failure. The political
  nightly service also rolls back earlier disclosures, policy activation and
  legislative results if its later executive election fails.
- Regional tier rebalancing now rolls back the entire direct batch, including
  earlier promotions and their history, when a later receipt fails.
- Raw regional and institutional action guidance checks both current residence
  and residence on the requested day. Outside people get no local-work menu;
  malformed history raises before actions or writes. Return uses the person's
  existing wallet and does not reinstate a released office.
- A first arrival chooses its initial contacts from current residents before
  applying the existing deterministic ranking. Prior social ties and outside
  people's identity, assets, claims and historical content remain intact.

Normal World NIGHT_CLOSE already owns a transaction, and ordinary action
execution already excludes outside actors. The new savepoints protect direct
service calls too. The stale-executive test deliberately retains the old input
snapshot; ordinary departure already clears the stored role. These distinctions
prevent the fault-injection evidence from overstating the normal runtime defect.

All changes are gated to the draft. The three extracted service bodies preserve
their original AST statements. Removing each new residence guard restores the
original context/arrival function AST. Old elections, ranking and random draws
retain their versioned contracts. Nightly retry remains idempotent; no new
idempotency promise is made for independently repeated direct election calls.
