# Each city has an isolated Civic Builder

Every city receives its own generated Civic Builder identity and isolated Citizen Runtime, credentials, memory, Builder Workspace, and Builder Mandate. Builders reuse a versioned Builder Skill Pack but never share private state or authority across cities. We accept duplicated runtimes and less cross-city experiential continuity to preserve tenant isolation, causal provenance, one-to-one agency, and independent city evolution.
