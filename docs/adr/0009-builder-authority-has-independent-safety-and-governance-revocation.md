# Builder authority has independent safety and governance revocation

The Builder Mandate may be suspended automatically after forbidden workspace access, invariant failure, policy-envelope breach, or repeated invalid actions; a human operator may revoke it immediately in an emergency; and ordinary performance review and succession proceed through in-world civic governance. Suspension stops new builder actions and quarantines unfinished expansion or proposal work without deleting the citizen or rewriting historical evidence. Builder Succession always creates a newly identified citizen and isolated runtime; public mandate records may carry forward, but private memory and authorship never transfer. The former builder remains an ordinary resident unless its citizenship is separately suspended under normal rules. Independent revocation paths prevent either city politics, operator absence, or an unsafe builder from becoming a single point of failure.
