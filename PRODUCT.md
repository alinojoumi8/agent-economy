# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are researchers and operators who run, observe, interrogate,
and control live Agent Economy simulations. They need to understand both the
state of the economy and the activity of individual AI agents without losing
the distinction between model proposals, validated actions, and settled
economic effects.

## Product Purpose

Agent Economy is an observable laboratory for studying how information,
beliefs, decisions, institutions, and markets interact inside a living
miniature economy. World OS is its operator interface: it should make the world
legible while it runs, support investigation across time and entities, and
preserve the simulator's research and replay guarantees.

The redesigned interface adds a live city experience where agents can be seen
working, moving, communicating, and producing economic events. Success means an
operator can orient to the world's current state immediately, identify active
agents and institutions, navigate to supporting evidence, and retain access to
all existing controls and workspaces.

## Positioning

The product joins persona-driven AI decisions to a deterministic economic
engine. Model outputs become bounded proposals; the engine validates and
settles consequences through auditable state, causal traces, and balanced
ledgers. The interface exposes that chain rather than presenting an opaque
agent spectacle.

## Operating Context

Users monitor long-running simulations, move between live and historical
ticks, inspect people and institutions, follow news and communications, trace
causal evidence, query the Oracle, inject authorized shocks, review provider
activity and cost, and replay or export runs. The classic Observatory and the
World OS workspace routes are both part of the current product surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing routes, controls, data access, run-state behavior, replay
  semantics, and error handling.
- Drive the city and activity views from real projection or observatory data
  whenever it is available.
- Any synthetic or fallback demonstration state must be visibly labeled and
  must not be presented as evidence from a real run.
- Keep live, stale, loading, historical, paused, failed, and empty states
  distinct.
- Preserve the boundary between current implementation, simulation output,
  forecasts, and unimplemented proposals.
- The city is an operational visualization and navigation surface, not a
  substitute for evidence-rich tables, inspectors, and causal views.

## Brand Commitments

The product names **Agent Economy** and **World OS** are retained. The identity
should communicate a living world and a rigorous research instrument together;
the redesign may replace the incumbent visual system and logo.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains live and historical projection APIs, World OS
workspaces, the classic Observatory, agent and institution inspectors, event
and communication streams, run controls, causal evidence views, provider and
cost status, replay support, and browser tests. Existing interface copy and
runtime data are product evidence; no customer, benchmark, commercial, or
deployment claims should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Make the living world visible without turning research state into theater.
2. Let every visual signal lead to inspectable evidence.
3. Keep orientation and navigation effortless during long-running sessions.
4. Make time, run state, agent activity, and system health unmistakable.
5. Preserve operator trust through truthful states and reversible controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must retain semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible
focus, reduced-motion behavior, sufficient contrast, readable density, and
responsive layouts. Motion and color cannot be the only indicators of agent or
system state.
